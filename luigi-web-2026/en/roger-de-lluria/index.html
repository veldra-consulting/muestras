The restaurant

A wooden bar and a wood-fired oven in the middle of the room: from the door you can already see where every pizza is made. This is the original Luigi, in the heart of the Eixample, a short walk from Passeig de Gràcia.

A warm, modern dining room with air conditioning, and a street terrace where dogs are welcome.
